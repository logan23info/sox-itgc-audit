Audit documentation

Evidence workpaper templates

Structured workpaper shells for each ITGC domain — formatted to match how audit teams document walkthroughs, testing of controls, and evidence review. Printable and field-ready.

PCAOB AS 2201 aligned Print-ready 5 domain templates Field-use ready
PCAOB requirement: Under AS 1215, audit documentation must be sufficient for an experienced auditor with no prior engagement connection to understand the procedures performed, evidence obtained, and conclusions reached. Documentation must be completed within 14 days of the report date.

What a workpaper must contain

  • Control objective being tested
  • Risk rating and assertion
  • Procedure performed
  • Population source and IPE validation
  • Sample selection methodology
  • Evidence obtained and evaluated
  • Exceptions noted
  • Conclusion on operating effectiveness
  • Preparer and reviewer sign-off

Common documentation deficiencies

  • IPE population not validated
  • Sample size not justified
  • Evidence not tied to specific control attribute
  • No written conclusion on effectiveness
  • Reviewer sign-off missing or undated
  • Exception not elevated to deficiency log
  • Workpaper completed after 14-day window

Sample size quick reference

  • Daily controls → 25 items min
  • Weekly → 10 items
  • Monthly → 3–5 items
  • Quarterly → 2 items
  • Annual → 1 item
  • High risk → uplift by 40–60%
  • New control → add 25% uplift
