Base samples before uplift factors. Always apply uplift for new controls (+25%), prior exceptions (+40%), or ITDM controls (+IPE validation step).

+25% uplift when:

  • Control is new or redesigned this year
  • Control owner changed during the period
  • Significant process change occurred

+40% uplift when:

  • Exceptions found in prior period testing
  • Elevated fraud risk indicators present
  • Control relies on judgement-heavy review

For ITDM controls, also:

  • Validate the IPE per AS 1105.10A first
  • Sample the human review step separately
  • Test the report parameters and completeness
