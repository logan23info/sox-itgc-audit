Key definitions — know these cold

The 10 definitions every SOX IT auditor must know

  • ITGC: Controls that apply to all IT systems and protect the infrastructure that application controls depend on
  • IPE: Information produced by the entity — system-generated reports used in controls that must be validated for completeness and accuracy
  • SoD: No individual can both initiate and approve a transaction (prevents concealment of errors or fraud)
  • Control deficiency: A gap in design or operation that doesn't meet the MW/SD threshold
  • Significant deficiency: Less severe than MW but important enough to communicate to the audit committee
  • Material weakness: Reasonable possibility of material misstatement not caught on a timely basis — requires public disclosure
  • Walkthrough: Tracing one transaction end-to-end to confirm design understanding per AS 2201.34
  • Operating effectiveness: The control operated as designed consistently throughout the audit period
  • Design effectiveness: The control, if operated properly, is capable of preventing or detecting a material misstatement
  • CUEC: Complementary User Entity Controls — controls the SOC 1 assumes your organization is operating

Numbers to memorize

  • Daily controls: 25 items sample (high risk: 30–40)
  • Weekly controls: 10 items sample
  • Monthly controls: 3–5 items
  • Quarterly controls: 2 items
  • Annual controls: 1 item
  • 14 days: Documentation completion deadline (AS 1215)
  • 4 business days: SEC 8-K deadline for material cybersecurity incidents
  • AS 2201: The PCAOB standard for integrated audits of ICFR
  • AS 2315: PCAOB standard for audit sampling
  • AS 1105.10A: IPE validation requirement
